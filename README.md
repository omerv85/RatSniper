# Rat Sniper

A night-vision sniper game for phones. You're a farmer defending the barnyard from rats, one 30-second night at a time.

**Play:** https://omerv85.github.io/RatSniper/

## How to play

- **Drag** to turn and aim. The crosshair stays in the middle of the screen.
- **Lift your finger** to fire.
- Each night lasts 30 seconds. Every 5th night is a boss round against the king rat.
- Head shots count as two hits.

Best played in landscape with the sound on.

## About

The whole game is a single `index.html` file: canvas graphics and Web Audio sound, all generated in code, with no images or audio files.
